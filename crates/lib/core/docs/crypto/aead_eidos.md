Eidos authenticated encryption for Felt messages and associated data.<br /><br />Each plaintext Felt m is split as m = lo + 2^32 * hi. Encryption XORs the two u32 limbs with<br />the keystream and stores them as two Felts. The encrypted limbs cannot always be packed into<br />one Felt: their 64-bit value may exceed the Goldilocks modulus. A message of n Felts therefore<br />needs 2*n ciphertext Felts, plus a two-Felt authentication tag.<br /><br />Associated data is authenticated but not encrypted. Use a fresh nonce for each encryption<br />under the same key. The ciphertext and tag match Rust's Eidos expanded-ciphertext format.<br />
