EcGFp5 point and field arithmetic for the two agreement implementations.<br /><br />Each agreement uses a fresh call context. R_X/Y hold the generator sum R; ACC_X/Y hold<br />the other accumulator. The preprocessed path sums Q multiples there; the direct path<br />uses it for the joint short-scalar check and then restores S before encoding the outputs.<br /><br />R_TERM_X/Y and TERM_X/Y hold the next points to add. Their packed layout matches a<br />multiplication-table entry. The direct path also fills TERM_X/Y from its own joint table.<br />Point addition here requires nonidentity subgroup points with distinct X coordinates;<br />the callers either establish this from scalar prefixes or handle exceptional cases first.<br />
